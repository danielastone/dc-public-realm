# Metro Utility Pilot Sources

Sources should be rechecked before field work because operating conditions can change.

## WMATA customer restrooms

https://www.wmata.com/ride/guides/restrooms.html

WMATA update dated August 31, 2026. It reports 36 stations with customer restrooms available for use and 17 with at least one accessible customer restroom. The page identifies station-specific assistance requirements and accessibility status and states that it will be updated monthly.

## WMATA accessibility

https://www.wmata.com/help/help-topics/accessibility-help/accessibility.html

WMATA states that rail stations provide step-free street-to-train access, including street-to-mezzanine and mezzanine-to-platform elevators.

https://www.wmata.com/ride/accessibility/metro-rail.html

Describes accessible entrance signage and elevator wayfinding.

## WMATA wayfinding redesign

https://www.wmata.com/initiatives/plans-and-projects/wayfinding.html

In September 2026 Metro began rolling out a revised rail wayfinding system intended to improve information for station entry, platforms, transfers, elevators, and exits.

https://www.wmata.com/initiatives/plans-and-projects/wayfinding/wayfinding-faq.html

The September 16, 2026 FAQ explains the exit-letter system and identifies accessible exits with the letter A.

## Elevator rehabilitation

https://www.wmata.com/initiatives/capital-improvement-program/upload/FY2026-Q1-Supplemental-Project-Pages.pdf

WMATA's FY2026 Q1 project pages describe the systemwide Elevator Rehabilitation Program and report completed or ongoing rehabilitation work at stations including Dupont Circle and Gallery Place.

## Data handling note

These sources establish operator-reported conditions at particular dates. The project should not copy a current operational condition into a timeless asset field. Store it as a dated observation and verify it again before publishing visitor-facing guidance.
