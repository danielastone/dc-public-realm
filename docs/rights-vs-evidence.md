# Image rights are not historical evidence

The project tracks two different provenance questions.

**Image provenance** asks whether a photograph may be reproduced and how it must be credited. Wikimedia Commons file pages, Creative Commons licenses, and explicit government public-domain statements answer that question.

**Claim provenance** asks whether a historical statement is supported, qualified, contradicted, or unresolved. That question is answered only through the assertion/evidence model.

A photograph's reusable license therefore does not promote the photograph or its caption into historical evidence. Conversely, an authoritative historical source does not automatically grant permission to republish its images.
