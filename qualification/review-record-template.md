## Evidence received

Identify the records, scans, citations, links, files, and archival context actually reviewed.

## Criteria applied

Record the decision on archival identity/authenticity, completeness, authority for each claim, temporal proximity, source dependency/inheritance, contradictions or qualifications, and reproducibility of the citation.

## Disposition

Choose one: `INCORPORATE`, `REJECT`, or `CLOSE_NO_CHANGE`.

## Authorized canonical changes

List the exact source, assertion, assertion-evidence, entity, predicate-rule, or research-task changes authorized by this review. If none, state `NONE`.

## Rationale

Explain why the evidence does or does not justify each authorized change. Preserve uncertainty and distinguish source custody from epistemic authorship.
